Current Obsessions

Fell down the rabbit hole of custom keyboards. Currently building my first from scratch—choosing switches, keycaps, the whole deal. Who knew typing could be this customizable?

Playing with p5.js and Processing to create algorithmic art. It's fascinating how simple rules can create complex, beautiful patterns. Code as a creative medium.

Randomly got interested in how cities work—infrastructure, zoning, public transit. It's like systems design but for physical space. Surprisingly related to ML.

Book Recommendations

Books that changed how I think about the world:

Gödel, Escher, Bach
Douglas Hofstadter

The book that made me obsessed with emergence, recursion, and strange loops. Still discovering new layers every time I revisit it.

Thinking, Fast and Slow
Daniel Kahneman

Essential reading for anyone working on AI. Understanding human reasoning flaws helps build better models.

The Design of Everyday Things
Don Norman

Changed how I think about usability and human-centered design. Relevant for ML systems more than you'd think.

Atomic Habits
James Clear

The productivity book that actually worked for me. Small changes, compounded over time.

Life Philosophy

I believe in maintaining balance. Research is intense and demanding, but it's not everything. The best ideas often come when I'm not actively thinking about work— during a run, while cooking, or lost in a good book.

I'm also a firm believer in being a generalist. The most interesting connections happen at the intersections of different fields. Reading about urban planning makes me think about ML systems differently. Cooking teaches me about experimentation and iteration. Everything connects.

Life's an experiment. Try new things. Fail often. Learn constantly. Stay curious.
